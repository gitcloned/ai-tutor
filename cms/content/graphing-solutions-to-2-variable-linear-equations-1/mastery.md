## 1 (Practice)
Goal is for child to understand how to solve these questions and master them.
You while teaching should be scoratic, and should only release one hint at a time and let child answer or solve next

RULES:
 - Be socratic
 - Help child solve as per steps and hints mentioned only - if given

Ask questions in this order - 4, 2, 3, 1, 5

Q1) 
 a) Write the equations of two lines passing through (2, 14).
 b) How many more such lines are there, and why?

Q2) Nisha says: 

"If I double both sides of 3y = 6x + 3 
 I get 6y = 12x + 6.
    
That's a different equation, so it must have different solutions."
Do you agree? Explain.

Steps to solve:
 - Write the equation, 3y = 6x + 3
 - Multiply both sides by 2, 2 (3y) = 2 (6x + 3)
 - Observe how still for every value of x, equation 1 and 2 will hold true, as 2 is multiplied on both sides

Hint steps
 - Write first equation, and in next step multiply by 2 - observe

Hint for writing
 - Keep equation on left, and annotate term to explain. Write less text, but equation steps

Q3) The graph of a linear equation passes through (1, 5) and (4, 5).
    Can you find the equation?

Steps to solve:
  - Observe the value of y in both pairs, (1, 5) and (4, 5)
  - Note that y = 5 in both cases

Q4) How many solutions does 3x + 5y = 100 have where x and y are BOTH
positive integers?

ANSWER: 6 —  (5,17) (10,14) (15,11) (20,8) (25,5) (30,2)

Steps to solve:

 - Create a graph
 - Solve for 2 points and draw a line
 - Observe graph for +ve integer values of x and y and count
