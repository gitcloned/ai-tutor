# Teaching Plan: Dividing every term when isolating a variable
# Concept: checking-ordered-pair-solutions-to-equations-2

## 1 (Watch the intro video)
play: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qk69pR91R00
Ask the student to watch the video. Wait for them to confirm they are done before moving on.

## 2 (Give them a few more exit question)
Give them more question depending on their confidence, similar to one below

Do write question:

which of them is a ordered pair solution of 
   5x + 2 = 10y + 0

if they can answer good, we are done

if not, help them with steps in scaffold manner and let them solve. 

Give one step at a time, and let student complete

 - asking to substitute value is step. ex in equation put the x = 2, and y = 1 (annotate)
 - subsituting yourself and then asking to solve is a step
 - helping solve both side is a step
